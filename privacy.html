EXPTRACKER · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

expTracker is a personal finance journal. Your records are stored on your device or in your browser. If you connect Google Drive, the app syncs a backup to that Google account’s private app-data area. Optional AI features send the content needed for your request to the AI provider you configure.

1. Information in expTracker

The app processes the information you enter, including income and expenses, balances and savings goals, diary entries, planned payments or purchases, calendar events, and reminder preferences.

When you sign in with Google, expTracker receives your Google account email and account identifier so it can associate your local records with the correct account and authorize Drive backup. Google handles sign-in and authorization under its own privacy terms.

2. Where your information is stored

On your device or in your browser

On Android and iOS, finance records are stored in an encrypted local database, with its key held in the operating system’s secure storage. In a supported web browser, finance records are encrypted in browser storage using Web Crypto. Data and saved settings remain tied to that device or browser; they are not automatically copied between devices unless you use Drive sync.

Google Drive backup

If you connect Google Drive, expTracker uses the limited drive.appdata permission to read and update its backup in Google Drive’s private app-data area. The backup is not shown as a regular file in Drive. It is sent over HTTPS as JSON; the app does not add end-to-end encryption to the Drive backup. Google’s own terms and privacy practices apply to its storage and processing.

Signing out does not delete a Drive backup. Use Google account access controls to revoke the app’s authorization. For help with a backup deletion request, contact the app operator below.

Exported files

If you export a backup, expTracker creates a JSON file for you to save. The file is not encrypted by the app. Choose a secure place to store it. API keys are not included in exported backups.

3. Optional AI features

AI is optional. You choose a provider and enter its API key in expTracker. The key is stored in the phone’s secure storage or encrypted browser settings and is not included in Drive backups or exported files. It is sent directly from your app or browser to the provider when you request an AI feature; the app operator does not receive it through an expTracker server.

  • Diary analysis: the diary text you ask the app to analyze is sent to your selected provider to produce suggestions. You review suggestions before adding them to your finance records.
  • AI assistant: your question and recent chat context are sent with relevant app context, such as recent transactions, diary excerpts, plans, upcoming events, balances, and savings totals.
  • Chat retention: chat messages are kept in memory during the current app session. expTracker does not save chat history to its finance database or Drive backup.

Your selected provider may process or retain requests under its own privacy policy and terms. Provider usage fees or limits may apply to your API key. Review the provider’s policies before enabling AI.

4. How information is shared

expTracker does not operate a separate server that stores your finance records. Information is sent to Google when you use Google sign-in or Drive sync, and to the AI provider you choose when you use an AI feature. The app does not sell your personal finance or diary records.

If this policy site is published using GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information, such as visitor IP addresses, for service security under its own policies.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or remove entries in the app, export your data, and disconnect Google. Local records remain on your device or browser until you remove the app’s data or clear that browser’s site storage. Signing out of Google removes the connection from the app but does not remove the Drive backup.

You can revoke Google access through your Google Account settings. To ask the app operator for help with a Drive backup or a privacy question, use the contact details below.

6. Security

The app uses encrypted local storage on supported devices and browsers and HTTPS for network requests. No storage method or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device, browser profile, and AI provider key.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page.
