Google sign-in identifies the account for your records. If you enable backup, expTracker uses Google’s drive.appdata permission to create, read, and update its own backup in that account’s private app-data area; it does not request access to your other Drive files. The backup can contain your finance records, diary entries, plans, and events. AI is optional and sends relevant content to your chosen provider only when you request an AI feature.
